What this is

Betta Arena is a website that shows what happens to the $BETTA token on Robinhood Chain: buys shown as fights, the ten biggest holders who never sold, and the buyback-and-burn rounds paid for with the token's creator fees. The site does not sell anything and never asks you to sign a transaction. $BETTA is bought and sold on Pons and other venues that are not run by this site.

A memecoin, not an investment

$BETTA is a memecoin. It has no promise of value, no yield, no dividend, no roadmap of returns and no team obligation of any kind. Buybacks spend creator fees only; past buybacks say nothing about future price. The token can lose all of its value. Only spend what you can afford to lose.

The fights and the shelf

Fights, fish, levels, the belt and the shelf are a display worked out from public chain data. They carry no money, no rights and no rewards. The rules may change, and the display may be wrong, late or offline.
