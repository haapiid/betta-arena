Privacy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

No accounts, no tracking

There is no sign-up, no cookie banner because there are no tracking cookies, and no analytics or advertising scripts on this site.

Your wallet

When you press Connect wallet, your wallet tells this page your public address. It is kept in your own browser (local storage) so the page can show your fish, your jar and your place on the shelf, and it is sent to our server only to look that public address up. The site never asks for a signature or a transaction, and never sees your keys. "Disconnect" forgets the address in your browser.

What the server sees

Like any web server, ours receives your IP address and browser details with each request. They are used to keep the service running and to stop abuse (rate limiting), and are kept only in short-lived server logs.

Public chain data

Everything the site shows (balances, buys, sells, fights, buybacks) comes from public data on Robinhood Chain, which anyone can read. We cannot change or remove it.

Third parties

Links to Pons, X, DexScreener and the block explorer take you to their sites, under their own privacy policies. Posting a fight card on X happens on X, with your X account.
